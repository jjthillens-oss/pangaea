# Nest-straw prop gate report

Status: **PROPOSED art candidate for review**. No production scene integration or final art approval is implied.

## Source and shape

The source is `nest-straw.blend`; `nest-straw-blockout.blend` preserves the gray shape stage. The deterministic original mesh recipe is in `build_nest.py`. The photographed reference and CC0 proof are in `PROVENANCE.md`; the photo was used for observation, not as mesh or texture input. The two sources of geometry are overlapping swept reeds and a concave basket web; the scene has no external mesh or image textures.

The gray three-quarter, front, and side PNGs were generated and visually inspected **before** the final material render. The first candidate read too much like a sparse ring. A bounded second pass added short crossing strands in the rim, inner cup and underside, varied rim curvature, and lowered the outward stalks. The final gray views were inspected before rebuilding material and export views. They show a low cup, a recessed opening, an uneven overlapping rim, and shorter outward stalks. The depth remains legible without color. The reviewed gray and material images are paired with the actual Godot screenshot in `comparison-sheet.png`.

## Visible differences and next decision

At gameplay distance the silhouette reads as a woven cup with short irregular projecting twigs and visible crossing strands. Compared with the CC0 photo, this candidate remains **shallower, brighter, and less densely interlaced**, especially in the inner well. The monochrome photograph cannot establish the real color. The warm palette is original design inference. The small open center and occasional wide gaps are visible in the top view. Review should decide whether this cleaner stylized reading suits Pangaea or whether a deeper, denser revision is needed before production use. Collision and integration are outside this asset PR.

## Reproduction

Run `build_nest.py` with Blender 5.2.2 LTS in two phases: `-- gray`, inspect the three gray PNGs, then `-- final`. Run `make_godot_review.ps1` with a new isolated directory, import with Godot 4.7.2, and run that project with `PANGAEA_CAPTURE` set to an output PNG path on a rendered desktop. `make_comparison_sheet.ps1` combines that capture with the reference and Blender views.
