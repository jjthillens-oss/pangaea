## Observed in the photograph

The plant has a tall, irregular central crown and shorter side crowns. Dense clusters of broad pointed leaves overlap around several upright stems; narrow holes remain between clusters. Upper leaves are deep bronze/purple, while lower leaves show muted green and olive. The image has bright washed background lighting, so exact reflectance and the hidden side are uncertain. We use low sheen and create coherent back-side branches rather than copying highlights or guessing unseen geometry.
