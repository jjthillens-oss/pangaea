# Quality sweep of the playable cell stage, 2026-10-23

Scope: `main` at d29cbce (M1 step 3 pool, title, settings, save slots, audio). Not in scope: the
endless-sea branches (s1, e2-*, e3-*, PR #69), art part meshes, the creature lab, and Godfrey's
issues #32 #33 #38 #39 #40 #53 #54 #55.

Baseline before any change: `tools/check.sh` green in the Linux container (611 tests, the M0
boot check, every smoke, the release pack). The sweep looked for what the gate does not catch.

## How it ran

Eight finder dimensions were planned (save/load, crash paths in scenes and UI, sim correctness and
determinism, game feel, performance, launch and Windows, input and settings, dynamic fuzz probes),
each to loop until two rounds found nothing new, with three skeptical verifiers per finding.
Three finders (save/load, crash paths, game feel) finished; the other five and all verifiers were
cut off by the account's weekly agent limit. The lead verified every finding by hand with
headless probes (Godot 4.7.2 `--headless --script`, isolated `XDG_DATA_HOME`) and by reading the
code and `docs/DECISIONS.md`. The five unfinished dimensions are listed under "Not yet covered".

Severity: crash (script error, hang, data loss) · bug (wrong behaviour a player can hit) · feel
(controls, pacing, readability) · perf · polish.

| 5 | feel | `game/ui/refusal_hints.gd` | The sim repeats a refusal every 0.5 s while contact lasts; the facing reason had no repeat interval, so plankton drifting against the cell's flank beeped twice a second (20 to 23 beeps in 20 s on seeds 4242 and 7). | `FACING_REPEAT_S` 4 s, like the diet hints. | 7 | polish | `game/scenes/main.gd` `death_text` | With fewer than 4 unspent points (the usual first death) the notice read "You lost a quarter of your unspent adaptation points (−0)". | Says there was too little to lose. Test added. |
