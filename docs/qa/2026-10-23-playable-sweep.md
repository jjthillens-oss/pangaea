# Quality sweep of the playable cell stage, 2026-10-23

Scope: `main` at d29cbce (M1 step 3 pool, title, settings, save slots, audio). Not in scope: the
endless-sea branches (s1, e2-*, e3-*, PR #69), art part meshes, the creature lab, and Godfrey's
issues #32 #33 #38 #39 #40 #53 #54 #55.

Baseline before any change: `tools/check.sh` green in the Linux container (611 tests, the M0
boot check, every smoke, the release pack). The sweep looked for what the gate does not catch.

## How it ran

Eight finder dimensions were planned (save/load, crash paths in scenes and UI, sim correctness and
determinism, game feel, performance, launch and Windows, input and settings, dynamic fuzz probes),
each to loop until two rounds found nothing new, with three skeptical verifiers per finding.
Three finders (save/load, crash paths, game feel) finished; the other five and all verifiers were
cut off by the account's weekly agent limit. The lead verified every finding by hand with
headless probes (Godot 4.7.2 `--headless --script`, isolated `XDG_DATA_HOME`) and by reading the
code and `docs/DECISIONS.md`. The five unfinished dimensions are listed under "Not yet covered".

Severity: crash (script error, hang, data loss) · bug (wrong behaviour a player can hit) · feel
(controls, pacing, readability) · perf · polish.

## Confirmed and fixed (this branch)

| # | Sev | Where | What happened | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | bug | `tools/check.sh` import step; `assets/export/sample_cell_T_PlayerCell_*.png` | A fresh import (every CI run, every new clone) re-extracted the three player cell textures from `sample_cell.glb` and rewrote them, so the tree was dirty after every check. Pixels identical; only the PNG encoding and generator md5 differed; regeneration is byte-stable. | Committed the regenerated bytes. |
| 2 | bug (data loss) | `game/systems/persistence_service.gd` `load_from` | `save_to` moves the primary to `.bak`, then `.tmp` into place. A crash between the two left no primary, the older save as `.bak` and the newest, verified save as `.tmp`; loading ignored `.tmp`, the title said the save could not be read, and the next save overwrote the newest state. Reproduced with a script. | A missing primary with a valid `.tmp` loads it (source `recovered`); nothing is moved; the next save fills the empty place. Test added. |
| 3 | bug (data loss) | `game/scenes/main.gd` `_quit`, `game/systems/settings_service.gd` | Settings were written only when the settings screen closed. Closing the window (or quitting from the pause menu's dialog) with the screen open lost every change made in it, with no dialog from the title. Reproduced: master volume 37 on screen, 80 on disk after the close. | `SettingsService.unsaved` tracks changes and rebinds; `Main._quit` writes the file when set. Test added. |
| 4 | bug | `game/scenes/main.gd` `delete_slot`, `_on_slots_closed` | Deleting the slot in use rebuilt the title under the open slots screen; its focus grab moved keyboard and controller focus to the hidden title, so the next Enter or A opened the body picker underneath, and closing the slots screen then left nothing focused. Reproduced with real key events. | The title is refreshed before the slots screen, so focus ends in the picker. Closing the slots screen with the title on another view returns to its main view. |
| 5 | feel | `game/ui/refusal_hints.gd` | The sim repeats a refusal every 0.5 s while contact lasts; the facing reason had no repeat interval, so plankton drifting against the cell's flank beeped twice a second (20 to 23 beeps in 20 s on seeds 4242 and 7). | `FACING_REPEAT_S` 4 s, like the diet hints. Test case added. |
| 6 | feel | `game/ui/hud.gd` | The controls panel (the only in-game place naming mouse swim, zoom, pause, full screen) hid at the first meal, about 2 s in on most seeds when the player swims at once. | It stays at least `CONTROLS_MIN_S` 12 s; a later first meal still hides it at once. |
| 7 | polish | `game/scenes/main.gd` `death_text` | With fewer than 4 unspent points (the usual first death) the notice read "You lost a quarter of your unspent adaptation points (−0)". | Says there was too little to lose. Test added. |
