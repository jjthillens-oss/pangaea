## Reading (first sweep, 2026-10-23, main at a9d0dba)

Status: PROPOSED. These are readings and tuning ideas, not changes; the gameplay code and data are untouched.

1. **A careful forager is never under pressure.** Grazer and omnivore under the wary and evade bots survive all 8 seeds with 95 to 96% mean energy and never get hungry. Food is plentiful near the spawn, so the only pressure in M1 is threats, and a player who reads tells is fully safe.
2. **Keeping an 8 m berth avoids threats almost entirely.** The wary bot (passes over food within 8 m of a creature, steers away inside 6 m) meets 0.10 to 0.23 threats a minute. Creatures notice from too short a range, or hold too still, to contest a cautious player.
3. **Dodging is near perfect.** EvadeBot's 0.4 s reaction dodges 147 of 148 tells at the grazer and every tell at the omnivore. The fairness contract promises the dodge, but there is no skill ceiling left above it.
4. **Rammers cause most deaths.** A careless grazer dies 2.25 times per 5 minutes (median first death 81 s), 14 of 18 to rammers; the omnivore 0.88, 5 of 7 to rammers. Ambush hunters kill rarely.
5. **Hunting is very rewarding.** HuntBot keeps the hunter alive in 8 of 8 seeds with 67 kills in 40 minutes (about 1.7 a minute) and 90% energy; the omnivore manages 55 kills and 93%. Rammers are easy prey from behind.
6. **The hunter has one way to live.** It eats only meat, and meat comes only from kills, so without hunting it starves about 3 times per 5 minutes. That is by design (plan D1), but a new player who picks the hunter and does not hunt starves inside about two minutes.

Tuning ideas for the engineering thread (each a data change and a re-run of this lab plus the step 3 gates):

- Slow plankton regrowth or raise upkeep a little so that a careful forager's mean energy lands near 70 to 80% rather than 95%.
- Lengthen notice range or add wandering so the wary bot meets at least 0.5 threats a minute.
- Give rammers a little more health or a faster turn so hunters kill fewer than one a minute.
- Add a starving tip for the hunter body ("Hunt rammers from behind for meat").
