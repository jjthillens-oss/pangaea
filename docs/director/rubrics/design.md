# Design rubric

Status: axes from Bible §2, §3, §4, §27 and the handbook (CONFIRMED sources); thresholds PROPOSED.

Score a feature from a scripted or recorded playthrough, not from the code. **Pass:** every axis 3 or more and a
mean of 4 or more.

| Axis | 1 (fail) | 3 (acceptable) | 5 (target) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tradeoff in play | A change with no downside, or an upgrade that is always right | Cost visible in numbers | Cost felt in play: you notice being slower, hungrier or spotted |
| Spore pillar | A pillar from the handbook part 2 missing or weaker than Spore's | Present, as good as Spore's | Clearly better than Spore's version |
| Bible layer | Bible idea ignored | Present but shallow | The Bible system drives choices |
| Continuity | Something resets or is lost across a save or era change | Carried over in the save | Carried over and visible in play |
| Next opportunity | Player cannot tell what to do next | A goal is shown | The world itself draws the player on |
| Honest scope | Mock buttons or later-era promises (Bible §24) | Only built features shown | Only built features shown, and each is complete |
| More than one way | One viable strategy | Two viable approaches | Approaches feel distinct (Bible §27 era rule) |
