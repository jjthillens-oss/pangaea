# Feel rubric

Status: drawn from the owner's playtests (handbook part 3, "Feel and pacing"); thresholds PROPOSED and tuned from
playtest data.

Score from a playthrough of at least 10 minutes. Measured targets come from the playtest capture where it can log
them. **Pass:** every axis 3 or more and a mean of 4 or more.

| Axis | 1 (fail) | 3 (acceptable) | 5 (target) | Measure |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pace | No meaningful choice in the first 2 minutes | First choice within 60 s | Within 30 s, then a new one every few minutes | time to first part, editor visits per 10 min |
| Tension | A size or area with no danger | Danger present at every size | Each size has a moment that makes you flinch | threat encounters per minute, near-deaths |
| Feedback | Hits, eats or level-ups pass unnoticed | Each has sound and a visual | Each lands with weight (anticipation, impact, reward) | review of frames and audio hooks |
| HUD | Clutter or missing state | Small HUD, readable at 1080p | Glanceable; nothing you need is hidden | HUD element count |
| Controls | Fighting the controls | Responsive | Feels good with nothing to think about | input-to-motion latency in ticks |
| Show a friend | Embarrassing | Fine | You want to show it off | reviewer's verdict, then the owner's |
