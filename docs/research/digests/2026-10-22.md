## New community signals

- **Steam Spore discussions, last 48 hours: support questions, not design asks.** Threads posted
  Oct 19 to 20 are about getting the game to launch, Civilization-stage help, and Spore account
  registration failing. This repeats the known "EA servers and log-ins" complaint (community-wants
  ref 14); nothing new on what fans want. https://steamcommunity.com/app/17390/discussions/
  (account thread: https://steamcommunity.com/app/17390/discussions/0/567048404945204512/)
- **"Spore 2" sentiment thread (posted Oct 7, newly surfaced for us).** The poster argues no studio
  has attempted Spore's scope since and asks people to "pray for a Spore 2"; replies say a sequel is
  unlikely because Maxis is gone. Supports the existing "no one fills this gap" signal; no new
  feature asks. https://steamcommunity.com/app/17390/discussions/0/587313969629376170/
- **Lost-progress / crash reports (early October).** A long-time player moving to a new PC hit an
  endless Creature-stage load; the reply's workaround is XP compatibility mode. Thread titles in the
  same week also mention no autosave and losing a game to a crash. Weak signal, but it points at
  crash-safe saves and portable save folders, which `community-wants.md` does not list yet.
  https://steamcommunity.com/app/17390/discussions/0/587313700791229693/
- **Thrive:** no new devblog or release since 1.6.0 (30 Aug, multicellular reproduction modes and
  early multicellular auto-evo). https://github.com/Revolutionary-Games/Thrive/releases (GPL,
  reference-only.)
- Reddit r/Spore could not be fetched from this environment, so it is not covered today.
