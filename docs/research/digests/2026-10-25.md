## New community signals

- **Steam Spore discussions: no new design asks.** The only thread active in the last day is a
  2024 roll-call ("We are here, we are waiting"); visible replies are check-ins plus one ask for
  working Sporepedia pictures, which repeats the known "online features broken" complaint.
  https://steamcommunity.com/app/17390/discussions/0/7091547412713876750/
- **"Spore 3 when??" (June 2026, newly surfaced for us).** Replies want any sequel but name no
  features; several warn that "Spore 2" trailers on YouTube are fake. Weak signal: a real
  successor will be met with suspicion, so honest in-engine footage matters for any later store
  page. https://steamcommunity.com/app/17390/discussions/0/572666820169865312/
- **Strange Seed (Chronicle Games Ltd), newly surfaced as a Spore-adjacent rival.** A creature
  evolution action game inspired by E.V.O. and Spore's creature stage: a blob eats creatures to
  take their DNA, 7 body-part slots, 40+ creatures, 10+ biomes. The Steam demo (Sept 2024) is
  "Very Positive" (91% of 109 reviews); PCGamesN calls it "a direct attempt at a successor".
  It is a fixed-slot part-swap game, not a free-form editor, so it confirms demand for
  eat-to-evolve without overlapping our seven-era scope.
  https://store.steampowered.com/app/3224660/Strange_Seed_Demo/ , https://www.pcgamesn.com/strange-seed/demo
- **Thrive:** no release or devblog since 1.6.0 (30 Aug). https://github.com/Revolutionary-Games/Thrive/releases
  (GPL, reference-only.)
- Reddit r/Spore is blocked from this environment, so it is not covered.
