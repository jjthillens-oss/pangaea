## New community signals

- **Thrive 1.7 announced (Devblog #54, posted 2026-10-24).** GPL, reference-only; design ideas only.
  Paraphrased: a new editor tab previews how the hex design becomes the in-game body and lets players
  place cells by hand (cells must stay connected and near their hex neighbours); reaching the next
  stage now needs more than a size count (cell count dropped to 15, plus minimum specialisation, an
  adjacency bonus, at least 3 cell types and a reproduction mode beyond budding, per the 10-10 update);
  ten stage achievements; AI species can make the multicellular jump on their own; a texture system
  for later-stage skins is in progress. The team openly debated whether the world should reset when
  the player leaves the editor and shipped it as an option, asking players which they prefer.
  https://www.revolutionarygamesstudio.com/devblog-54-the-achievementing ,
  https://www.revolutionarygamesstudio.com/progress-update-10-10-2026
  Relevance: supports our S4 editor work (preview of the played body inside the editor) and the
  Bible's "earned transitions" idea; the reset-on-exit question is one we will face at S4 too.
  Thrive's GitHub releases page still lists 1.6.0 as latest at access time.
  https://github.com/Revolutionary-Games/Thrive/releases
- **Steam Spore discussions:** no new design asks in the last 48 hours. The busiest thread ("Calling
  all the spore players in 2024-2026", active within 9 h) is greetings; one player says they keep
  making creatures until sharing "becomes sharable again", which repeats the known online-sharing
  complaint. https://steamcommunity.com/app/17390/discussions/0/7091547412713876750/
- No Spore-successor press news found. Reddit r/Spore not fetched.
