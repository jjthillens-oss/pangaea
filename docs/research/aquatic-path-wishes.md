# What players want from an underwater path through the whole game

**Status: PROPOSED research, not approved design.** This brief records what players have asked for, from public
pages only. It changes nothing in the Bible, the era count or the milestone scope. The companion draft
`docs/bible/addendum-aquatic-path.md` turns it into a Bible addendum that the owner approves or rejects.

- **Date:** 2026-10-25 (all sources accessed on this date)
- **Author:** research thread, Pangaea
- **Owner's brief:** "research what the players want for an aquatic underground, like underwater world with cities
  and civilization ... update the Bible to make sure that the aquatic stage is building that out."
- **Companions:** `docs/stages/aquatic-plan.md` (the built Aquatic stage and its section 7a exit plan, written by the
  Aquatic stage thread), `docs/proposals/aquatic-era.md`, `community-wants.md` themes 6 and 12, `mod-wishes.md`
  wish 6 and wish 12, `innovation-backlog.md` IB-07 and IB-08 and decision D2.

## Method and boundaries

**Read:** 6 Steam discussion threads for Spore and Thrive, 2 EA forum threads, 1 CivFanatics thread (2008), 2 Reddit
discussions via read-only mirrors, 1 Lemmy/kbin thread, 6 Thrive community forum threads and the Thrive FAQ and
wiki, 2 Elysian Eclipse devlogs, the Spore wiki's Aquatic Stage and Removed Features pages, Wikipedia's Development
of Spore, unseen64's beta page, and for comparison games: a PC Gamer review of Subnautica, a PC Gamer review and an
aggregated Steam-review summary (vaporlens) of Aquatico, and one Aquatico Steam thread. 13 web searches.

**Not done:** No game files, mods or binaries were opened. Spore's cut content is described only from secondary
sources and quoted developer statements. Nothing is copied: GENESIS reuses no Spore name, part, layout or system.

**Ranking:** one count per distinct poster, thread, devlog or review that raises a wish. Strength is high when the
wish recurs across communities (Spore, Thrive, Elysian Eclipse, city-builder players) or carries a popularity
signal. The underwater-civilization topic is a minority wish in raw numbers but an unusually durable one: it has
recurred for 20 years, from the 2005 GDC demo to 2024 Steam threads, and Thrive's forum banned the topic twice
because it would not die.

## What the sources say that the counts do not

1. **The sea stage is the single most-mourned cut, and it is already being built.** Rank 1 is settled for GENESIS:
   `docs/stages/aquatic-plan.md` §8a is implemented. The new asks are ranks 2 to 10: what happens *after* the sea
   stage if the player never leaves the water.
2. **Spore planned exactly what Joshua is asking for, then cut it for usability, not for lack of demand.** The
   2005 plan had underwater tribes, cities and vehicles; Will Wright said it would be "among the first things added"
   in an expansion [A8][A9]. That expansion never came. Twenty years of forum posts since are, in effect, the
   backlog for it. This is a design gap, not a design mistake: no mainstream game has shipped a creature-to-space
   path that stays wet.
3. **The science fight is the design brief.** Thrive's community spent six threads and two bans arguing whether
   an underwater people can get past the stone age. Every "problem" they raised is a gameplay hook for GENESIS:
   heat comes from vents, not fire; metal is scarce and precious, so shell, bone, stone and living material carry
   the early game; power comes from currents and electric organs; light is a resource; food must be farmed and
   penned because it cannot be stored dry; the first "writing" is carved, grown or sung. The Bible already asks
   for understandable tradeoffs (§2) and tools as extensions of anatomy (§9), and it says "do not simply prohibit
   advancement for creatures without human hands." A sea people without fire is the same rule applied to a habitat.
4. **The two escape hatches fans accept are both good GENESIS content.** An amphibious or shore society (rank 4)
   is the era 3 shore gradient extended into eras 5 and 6: cities straddle the tideline with wet and dry quarters.
   Uplift or trade with a land people (rank 10) is Bible §10 diplomacy with a second culture on the same world.
   Neither needs a new era.
5. **City-builder players tell us what an underwater city must feel like.** Aquatico scored 36% positive: players
   loved the setting, the drifting sea life and the resource tension, and hated that the city felt like pipes and
   factories, that domes were cut off from industry, and that the tech tree was a flat scroll [A18][A20].
   Subnautica players loved the base becoming "a personal space" and the world becoming familiar [A19]. For
   GENESIS: an underwater settlement must read as a living reef, not a refinery; homes, farms and workshops mix;
   light and life are visible from inside.
6. **Navigation is the production risk, not the fantasy.** The stage was cut over 3D navigation, camera and
   animation [A8][A11]. The Aquatic stage plan §4 already addresses movement for one fish; settlements add
   placement, pathing for workers in three dimensions and a camera that must show depth. The addendum asks for
   these to be proven on one settlement before any content expansion (Bible §23: expand one validated loop).

## Scope notes

- Adding aquatic play to eras 3 to 7 is a **content scope change**. The Bible permits it in principle (§4: "offer an
  accessible evolutionary route, allow continued aquatic play within supported scope"; "alternative paths require
  bounded content plans") and requires the owner's approval of the bounded plan. The addendum is that plan.
- The Bible's exclusions are untouched: no multiplayer, live service or user scripting is involved.
- Realism (rank 12) is a tone choice. The Bible's CONFIRMED stylised target and its "understandable tradeoffs"
  pillar favour the "vents, shells and bred creatures" path over hard exclusion. The addendum states this as a
  design position for the owner to confirm, not as fact about real-world feasibility.
- Nothing here changes era count, era order or the M1 to M4 milestones. It adds aquatic variants to M5 to M8 and a
  spike before M5.
