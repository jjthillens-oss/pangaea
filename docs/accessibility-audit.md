# Accessibility audit — microscopic stage

**Status: PROPOSED fixes.** Audited `main` at `97b929b` against GENESIS Bible v0.1 §19 (PDF p. 14), 2026-10-23. This is a source and asset audit, not a rendered playtest. PR #28's menus and settings are already present on this `main`; findings below reflect them rather than treating that work as pending. Severity describes the impact of the current build, not a promise about later eras.

## Color and cue method

I inspected the RGB source values in `tools/blender/lookdev.py:27-30`, `docs/art/ART_DIRECTION.md:42-54`, `game/scenes/pool/creature_view.gd:62-69`, `game/scenes/pool/threat_lane.gdshader:30-32`, and `assets/export/M_VeilThreadInk.tres:8-10`. I converted sRGB channels to linear light, applied full-severity 3×3 protanopia, deuteranopia, and tritanopia simulation matrices, clipped to 0–1, then converted back to sRGB. Contrast below is the WCAG relative-luminance ratio of the *simulated solid source colors*. This is a screening calculation: baked textures, emission, lighting, fog, transparency, and tone mapping can alter the visible result. Verify with captures from the game under all three filters before changing palette values.

| Pair / source | Normal | Protanopia | Deuteranopia | Tritanopia |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Food glow `#CFE86A` vs threat `#D9466B` | 3.06:1 | 4.01:1 (`#F5DD5D` / `#69696C`) | 2.64:1 (`#F2DD72` / `#8F8767`) | 3.01:1 (`#D9DDCC` / `#EC2B55`) |
| Food glow vs fluid deep `#0B2E6B` | 9.52:1 | 8.80:1 | 9.70:1 | 8.62:1 |
| Threat vs fluid deep | 3.12:1 | 2.19:1 | 3.68:1 | 2.87:1 |

These ratios are a diagnostic, not a text contrast pass/fail criterion for 3D meshes. Threat markers already use `!`, jaws/stars, outlines, and a directional lane (`game/ui/threat_indicators.gd:26-32,98-108`; `game/scenes/pool/threat_lane.gdshader:5-13`), so the palette is not the only signal. The food meshes are different forms—plankton's radial diatom and carrion's torn chunk (`assets/records/plankton.json:5`, `assets/records/carrion.json:5`)—and the HUD describes diet in words (`game/ui/hud.gd:266-275`).

## Findings and fixes

| Severity | §19 requirement | Current evidence | Concrete fix |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Medium** | Use shape, animation, labels, or audio alongside color; critical danger must not rely on color discrimination. | The threat source `#D9466B` against deep fluid `#0B2E6B` falls to 2.19:1 in the protanopia screening calculation. The on-screen `!` and lane have non-color form (`game/scenes/pool/creature_view.gd:62-69`, `game/scenes/pool/threat_lane.gdshader:5-13`), but the thin threat line and spike material (`assets/export/M_VeilThreadInk.tres:8-10`) can be hard to see in isolation. | In a rendered color-vision review, check the lane and spikes on dark and lit backgrounds at gameplay size. Increase line width or add a persistent high-luminance edge/pattern while retaining the existing `!` and shape; test the three filters and ordinary vision. |
| **Medium** | Provide readable text scaling. | Settings offer 100/115/130% (`game/domain/game_settings.gd:35`, `game/ui/settings_menu.gd:39`) and scale theme sizes (`game/systems/settings_service.gd:286-305`). The world-space starving arrow draws at a fixed `FONT_SIZE := 22` (`game/scenes/pool/starving_arrow.gd:18,124-126`), bypassing that scale. The theme's `SmallLabel` is 22 at 100% (`game/ui/theme/pangaea_theme.tres:506-507`). | Apply the chosen text scale to manually drawn cues and recompute their outlines, boxes, and safe areas. At 130%, inspect HUD, arrows, menus, and notices at 1080p and lower supported resolutions for clipping and overlap. |
| **Medium** | Provide a setting for flashing effects. | `Reduce flashing effects` exists (`game/ui/settings_menu.gd:41`) but the only checked use is HUD banner pulse strength (`game/ui/hud.gd:427-433`). Player and rival hit overlays still jump to near-full red/white-red for 0.15 s (`game/scenes/pool/player_cell_view.gd:40-41,280-293`; `game/scenes/pool/creature_view.gd:50-53,514-520`). The hurt vignette avoids a full-screen flash (`game/ui/hurt_vignette.gdshader:3`). | Make the reduction preference cap or replace the player and rival hit flashes too; preserve a nonflashing hit cue such as a brief shape/outline and text. Check repeated hits and the enabled/disabled setting in a captured sequence. |
| **Low** | Provide reduced camera motion and adjustable camera control. | The pool camera has no shake, roll, or FOV kick, but follows with a 0.12 s half-life and zoom easing (`game/scenes/pool/pool_camera.gd:3-9,18-24,48-59`). The setting reduces title-world motion (`game/ui/start_screen.gd:145,150`) and menu bounce (`game/ui/menu_bounce.gd:24`), not pool follow. Zoom speed and inversion are adjustable (`game/domain/game_settings.gd:31-33`; `game/scenes/main.gd:421-429`). There is no free-look camera to sensibly apply mouse sensitivity to in this stage. | Test follow/zoom comfort with the reduced-motion preference. If necessary, offer an immediate or less elastic pool camera follow/zoom option; document that look sensitivity is not applicable to the fixed camera. |
| **Low** | Display inputs from bindings and support remappable keyboard/mouse plus planned controller use. | Gameplay action bindings are edited by keyboard or controller and applied to `InputMap` (`game/ui/settings_menu.gd:8-10,150-176`; `game/systems/settings_service.gd:182-206,226-260`). HUD controls use `KeyLabel` (`game/ui/hud.gd:249-263`). Yet the settings footer hardcodes `Q / E`, `A / B`, and `Esc` (`game/ui/settings_menu.gd:230-237`); the title hint also hardcodes movement words (`game/ui/start_screen.gd:153-157`). | Derive all displayed inputs from the current action bindings or identify fixed menu-navigation inputs as intentionally non-remappable. Add a test that rebinds an action and checks each affected hint. |
| **Low** | Opening a menu must not leave a dangerous action held. | Pool unhandled input stops while paused (`game/scenes/pool/pool.gd:218-230`); focus loss requests pause (`game/scenes/pool/pool.gd:233-235`). This is a good guard, but the source audit does not prove that a held swim/attack input is cleared on resume. | Add an automated hold–pause–release–resume input test and require a fresh press before a dangerous action resumes. Exercise both keyboard and controller. |
| **Deferred** | Subtitles for spoken content; relevant, dismissible/revisitable prompts and a journal. | The settings text says subtitles arrive with the first spoken lines (`game/ui/settings_menu.gd:49`). Current cues are effects/music; `game/scenes/audio/cell_audio.gd` provides sound tells, with visual threat cues in `game/ui/threat_indicators.gd`. HUD refusal hints time out (`game/ui/hud.gd:158-163,344`) and input help appears in the pause menu (`game/ui/pause_menu.gd:39-60`). | When spoken lines are introduced, pair every line with timed, scalable subtitles; retain a way to revisit dismissed tutorial information. Plan an objective/discovery journal before later-era scope is approved. |

## Positive coverage and follow-up

The current UI pass implements much of §19: settings persistence and validation (`game/domain/game_settings.gd:20-39`), controller-capable remapping (`game/systems/settings_service.gd:20-30,182-206`), binding-aware controls (`game/ui/key_label.gd:3-5`), text scale, reduced title/menu motion, and a flashing preference. Threats have directional markers and explicit warning shapes; food and damage feedback also have words (`game/scenes/pool/feedback_fx.gd:4-8`). These should be retained while addressing the gaps.

This audit did not capture rendered frames or run a user study. The next validation pass should capture the pool at food and threat distances with normal, protanopia, deuteranopia, and tritanopia filters; compare 100% and 130% text at supported resolutions; record repeated-hit sequences with reduced flashing enabled; and verify keyboard/controller remaps and held-input transitions. No code or asset change is proposed by this document itself.
