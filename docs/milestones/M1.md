## Step 1: rules (game/domain)

| File | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| `phenotype.gd` | The one stat calculation. Benefits diminish per extra copy (copies ranked largest first, so part order never matters); upkeep and mass are paid in full and scale with size; mass slows speed and acceleration; stats are capped. Keeps mouth and spike angles, and each spike's own damage, for facing checks. |
| `actor_state.gd` | Health and energy. Upkeep and movement drain energy; at zero the cell moves at half speed and loses health, and food ends it. Health regenerates above half energy. |
| `interaction_resolver.gd` | Feed, bite and ram: diet, reach, facing arc, size limit and cooldown. Only spikes facing the target count in a ram (strongest first, then diminishing); backing away is no ram; nothing can bite or ram itself. Damage includes the target's defense, applied once. A refusal names the missing requirement and changes nothing. |
| `ecology_region.gd` | Plankton zones with capacity and logistic regrowth plus a floor, so an empty zone always recovers. Seeded spawn points. Niche caps by role. |
| `editor_session.gd` | Working copy with undo, redo, mirror, move, scale; cost of additions minus refunds; preview equals commit; a failed commit keeps the original body and points. |
| `lineage.gd` | Found a lineage from a starting body (grazer, hunter, omnivore); reproduction needs 70% energy and a session opened with the save's current points, and records generation n+1; death keeps the body and loses a quarter of unspent points; points are earned from food and one per 30 s alive. |
| `save_game.gd` + `systems/save_migrations.gd` | Save schema v2 adds adaptation points; v1 saves migrate in memory. A missing or wrong-typed field in a save (`json_values.gd`) fails validation, so the backup is used and is not overwritten by the damaged file. |

All numbers are PROPOSED tuning (`game/data/part_catalog.json` and the constants at the top of each file).

## Step 2: swim and eat (the first playable pool)

**State:** IMPLEMENTED. The owner played a preview build on Windows; the checklist below is not recorded yet.
The final pre-build plan is kept in `docs/milestones/m1-step2-plan.md`; "As built" below lists where the build differs.

### What the player gets
- The game opens on a start screen: Continue, New lineage and Quit, with the build id at the bottom right. The Continue card shows the body, lineage tag, generation, points, saved time and a preview of the cell.
- New lineage shows three body cards: grazer, hunter and omnivore. Each has a role, a diet and stats taken from the rules: speed, turning, upkeep, sight and max energy. One shared preview shows the selected body.
- The tide pool is one round pool 80 m across with 18 rocks and 7 plankton zones. The cell swims on one flat plane. The camera looks down at 55 degrees, always faces north and follows with a short look-ahead. The mouse wheel zooms between 10 and 20 m.
- Controls: WASD or the arrow keys swim in screen directions; holding the left mouse button swims toward the cursor. Esc or P pauses. F11 toggles fullscreen. Eating happens on contact; there is no eat key.
- Two foods. Plankton drifts inside its zone and regrows from the zone's stock; a filter mouth eats it. Carrion lies still and rots after 120 s; a jaw eats it. The grazer has a filter mouth, the hunter a jaw, the omnivore both.
- For now carrion also appears by itself in the zones. This is interim: the plan is for step 3 to turn it off, so carrion comes only from kills.
- Every meal goes through the step 1 rules. When food touches the cell but cannot be eaten, a hint says why: "Needs a jaw: your filter mouth only eats plankton", "Needs a filter mouth: your jaw only eats carrion", "Turn your mouth toward it" or "Full". With a diet hint the cell also shakes its head. Every hint except "Full" plays a sound.
- HUD: an energy bar with a Fed, Hungry or Starving chip; a health bar while hurt or starving; the lineage line; adaptation points with a +N flash; meal toasts such as "+8 energy · +1 point"; one survival point per 30 s alive; a controls hint until the first meal or for 30 s.
- Starving: a banner with an icon and text, half speed, the health bar, a grey cell and an arrow to the nearest food this body can eat.
- Death (interim): an "Out of energy" notice says the lineage continues and names the points it costs (a quarter of the unspent points). Continue puts the cell back at the start with full health. The world keeps going.
- Pause menu: Resume, Save, Save and quit to title, Quit to desktop. Quitting or closing the window with unsaved changes asks "Save first?". The footer shows the seed and build.
- Continue brings back the lineage, body, generation, points and place. Energy and health are not saved, so the cell comes back rested (60% energy, full health) and the HUD says so.
- Placeholder sounds are made in code at startup (eating, refusal, a starving pulse, ambience). Every sound except the ambience has a visual cue.
- Blender blockouts for the pool floor, three rocks, plankton, carrion and four parts (filter mouth, jaw, flagellum, eye spot). Each has a `.blend`, a GLB and a record. They are PROPOSED and not visually approved.

**Dev build only** (`Pangaea-dev.exe` and runs from the editor; none of this is in `Pangaea.exe`):
- F3 opens the debug panel: build, ids, seeds, tick, step cost, FPS, draw calls, adapter, driver, actor and derived stats, the last feeding results, zones and carrion.
- F5 fills energy, F6 drains it, F7 empties the nearest zone, F8 drops carrion 4 m ahead. The panel also has Teleport to spawn, gizmos (zones, reach, mouth arcs) and a vsync toggle.
- `-- --perf-tour` lets the forage bot swim for 90 s after a 2 s warm-up, then writes `perf_<build>.json` to `reports/` in the user folder and quits.

### How it is built

| Layer | Files | Job |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `game/domain` | `plane_space.gd`, `locomotion.gd`, `pool_arena.gd` | The plane mapping, swimming from the body's stats (turn, then thrust; sideways drift damped; total speed never above the top speed), the soft wall, sliding along rocks and the hard edge. |
| `game/domain` (changed) | `interaction_resolver.gd`, `lineage.gd`, `save_game.gd` | "not hungry" when the cell has room for less than half the food's energy; `starting_body_of` names a starting body; `death_loss` previews the death penalty; a save refuses a world seed above 2^53. |
| `game/systems` | `pool_sim.gd` | The whole game at a fixed 60 ticks a second: swimming, metabolism, survival points, ecology, feeding through the resolver, and events. The same seed and inputs give the same world. |
| `game/systems` | `plankton_field.gd`, `carrion_field.gd`, `pool_layout.gd` | Plankton items as a view of zone stock; carrion, rotting and the interim supply; the layout data and its checks. |
| `game/systems` | `game_session.gd`, `run_flags.gd`, `sim_intent.gd` | New lineage, continue, save and the interim respawn; launch flags; the swim input for one tick. |
| `game/systems` (changed) | `persistence_service.gd` | A save counts as written only when the temp file loads back with the same lineage and seed. |
| `game/scenes` | `main.tscn`, `pool/`, `audio/` | `main.tscn` is the main scene and switches between the start screen and the pool. The pool steps the sim once per physics tick, draws it smoothly between ticks, and plays effects and sounds for sim events. |
| `game/ui` | start screen, body cards, cell preview, HUD, pause menu, notice dialog, key labels, refusal hints, body text, theme | Every number shown comes from the rules. Key names come from the physical key bindings. |
| `game/dev` | debug panel, gizmos, dev keys, forage bot, perf tour | Debug builds only. `main.gd` loads them by path when `OS.is_debug_build()` is true. |
| `assets` | 10 blockouts; `records/placeholders/inventory.json` | The inventory lists the synthesized sounds (to be replaced in step 5) and the functional primitives (eat-burst particles, labels, dev gizmos). A test fails on any mesh in game code that is not listed. |
| `tests` | 16 new suites, `smoke/play_smoke.gd`, two pool fixtures | 162 new tests (252 in all) and the two-process scene smoke. |

### As built: differences from the plan that matter

| Area | Plan | As built | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Carrion supply | Rots at 90 s; at most 6 live; one every 9 ± 3 s | Rots at 120 s; at most 12 live; one every 10 ± 3 s | With the plan's numbers, a hunter starving from the rim right after the first carrion rotted missed food in a third of runs. Now the worst measured miss rate is 5% over 60 seeds, and a late-run test holds it. About 11 pieces are live when nobody eats (peak 12). |
| Pool floor | Three materials assigned per face; floor to r 58 | One material, `M_PoolFloor`, coloured per vertex, so sand, silt and rim stone fade into each other; floor to r 66 (17,536 triangles) | The silt patches had stair-step edges. From the wall at full zoom-out the view reaches about r 64.9. |
| Eye spot | Sits at y 0.18 | Sits at y 0.30 on the true surface normal (`PartPlacement.RAISED_Y`) | At 0.18 the grazer's eye spot was inside the sample cell's baked right eye. |
| Rocks | The widest point within 15% of the 1.0 m footprint | Every direction within 15%, across the swim band and in the swim plane (0.92 to 1.05 m); top 0.58 m at scale 1 | Parts of the outline were 20% inside the collision circle, so the cell stopped short of the visible rock. |
| Refusal events | Repeat only when the food or reason changes, or every 0.5 s | Also reported on the tick contact begins. Only the nearest refusal is reported, and only on ticks with no meal. | A brief touch of food the cell cannot eat often got no hint, head shake or sound. |
| Starving events | (not specified) | `starving_ended` also fires when a starved cell respawns, before `respawned` | Starting and ending now always alternate. |
| Continue position | A saved point must be clear of rocks | Clear within 0.1 mm (`PoolArena.CLEAR_EPSILON` 1e-4) | Float rounding made a cell saved against a rock continue from the spawn about 2% of the time. |
| Launch flags | `--seed=N`, `--slot=PATH` | `--seed` takes 0 to 4294967295; `--slot` must name a file under `user://` or an absolute path; refused flags are reported and ignored. Saves refuse a world seed above 2^53. | Huge seeds saved "successfully" and then could not load; folder-like slots wrote stray files. |
| Speed while turning | Forward speed capped | Total speed capped: thrust stops at the square root of (top speed² − sideways speed²) | Turning carried the cell a few percent past its top speed. |
| Plankton drift | Drift of 0.35 m on each axis | Anchors stay 0.495 m (0.35 × √2) inside the zone edge; spawns keep that much plus the item radius clear of rocks | Drift could carry an item out of its zone. |
| Zone empty and regrown | The empty flag re-arms at half capacity | Re-arms at half capacity or one plankton unit, whichever is more; a zone smaller than one unit is refused | Small zones reported empty and regrown over and over. |
| Continue heading | Face the nearest zone | The same, except a saved position equal to the spawn keeps the spawn heading | The sim cannot otherwise tell a new run from a continue. |
| Pool events | Drained once per tick | Also drained before each frame is drawn | Found by the smoke: above 60 Hz, a respawn or teleport could draw a frame with the camera at the old place. |
| Fog and light | Depth fog | Exponential fog; lighting retuned | Depth fog had no visible effect in the Compatibility renderer. |
| Smoke | Found, starve, save, continue | Also the F3 panel, a death and respawn, the quit dialog, and a per-frame probe (cell on screen, food counts, energy bar) | Covers more of the player path. The saved points include the death penalty (4 − 1 = 3). |

Smaller changes: the death notice's title is "Out of energy"; the body card says "Max energy"; the cell preview sways instead of spinning; HUD toasts show as well as the world-space meal labels; `CarrionField.spawn` takes its energy and points from the data (a 16-energy kill gives 2 points); dev commands report whether they applied; the placeholder scan also catches saved, referenced and binary meshes and model files under `game/`.

### Known issues
- **Carrion density.** About 11 pieces are live when nobody eats, above the readability guide of about 8. A "spread" placement (every zone gets one before any gets two) is proposed, not built. The plan is for step 3 to turn the interim supply off.
- The starving arrow and the forage bot aim at the nearest edible item, even one that will rot before the cell arrives.
- A nearer item that is not touching can hide a refusal for a farther item that is touching (it needs food of both sizes close together; rare).
- `test_refusals_are_reported_sparingly_and_never_for_quiet_reasons` checks a plain 0.5 s spacing that the code does not promise. It passes on its fixed seed; another seed or a data change could trip it while the behavior is correct.
- Quitting without saving after a death skips the death penalty until step 4 autosave. Continue gives 60% energy and full health, so saving and continuing ends starvation; the HUD says the cell rested.
- A cell wedged between two rocks could still overlap one by up to 1 mm and continue from the spawn. The real layout's rock gaps are at least 6.7 m, so this cannot happen there.
- The rule that rocks stay 1 m clear of zone edges is a review-time check, not a test.
- Look: the floor reads pale and the underwater feel is weak. Lighting was tuned only in the Compatibility renderer under Xvfb. All blockouts are below the visual bar (see `docs/DECISIONS.md`).
- The floor's vertex colours are stored sRGB-encoded in COLOR_0, so other glTF viewers show the floor lighter than the game does.
- Mouths sit on the body's equator, so from the 55 degree camera they are partly hidden when the cell faces north.
- The death pose is subtle at 14 m; the death notice carries the message.
- Food does not block the cell, so carrion a grazer cannot eat can draw overlapping it.
- Within about 1.5 m of food, the starving arrow stops at its least distance from the cell, so its head can sit on the food it points to. Its label, then beside the head, can cover other plankton nearby.
- The filter mouth's fringe looks like small spikes; it may need to look different from the step 4 spike.
- An eye spot clears the baked eyes only up to about scale 1.2; the step 4 editor allows 2.0. The eye spot's and filter's centres clear their asset-test bound by only 10 to 15 mm.
- The asset scans cover `game/` and `assets/export/` only. A model file outside both, or a preload of one, is not caught.
- No console wrapper for `Pangaea-dev.exe`, because `setup_toolchain.sh` installs only the two Windows templates. Console output is not visible on Windows.
- The debug panel covers the HUD's points and toasts when open, and its text is about 12 px at 1280×720. Dev only.
- A headless perf tour reports no adapter and a 0×0 resolution. Real numbers need a windowed run of `Pangaea-dev.exe`.
- The bundled font has no ◆ or ⚠ glyphs; the HUD draws them (`HudIcon`).
- Screenshot helpers are not in the repository yet; the recipe is in `docs/TOOLCHAIN.md`.
- Lineage and genome ids differ between smoke runs, because ids are not seeded. Apart from those ids, the save time and the build id, the PASS lines are the same in every run.
