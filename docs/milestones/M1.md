## Step 1: rules (game/domain)

| File | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| `phenotype.gd` | The one stat calculation. Benefits diminish per extra copy; upkeep and mass are paid in full; mass slows speed and acceleration; stats are capped. Keeps mouth and spike angles for facing checks. |
| `actor_state.gd` | Health and energy. Upkeep and movement drain energy; at zero the cell moves at half speed and loses health, and food ends it. Health regenerates above half energy. |
| `interaction_resolver.gd` | Feed, bite and ram: diet, reach, facing arc, size limit and cooldown. A refusal names the missing requirement and changes nothing. |
| `ecology_region.gd` | Plankton zones with capacity and logistic regrowth plus a floor, so an empty zone always recovers. Seeded spawn points. Niche caps by role. |
| `editor_session.gd` | Working copy with undo, redo, mirror, move, scale; cost of additions minus refunds; preview equals commit; a failed commit keeps the original body and points. |
| `lineage.gd` | Found a lineage from a starting body (grazer, hunter, omnivore); reproduction needs 70% energy and records generation n+1; death keeps the body and loses a quarter of unspent points. |
| `save_game.gd` + `systems/save_migrations.gd` | Save schema v2 adds adaptation points; v1 saves migrate in memory. |

All numbers are PROPOSED tuning (`game/data/part_catalog.json` and the constants at the top of each file).
