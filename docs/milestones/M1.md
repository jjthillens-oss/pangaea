## Step 1: rules (game/domain)

| File | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| `phenotype.gd` | The one stat calculation. Benefits diminish per extra copy (copies ranked largest first, so part order never matters); upkeep and mass are paid in full and scale with size; mass slows speed and acceleration; stats are capped. Keeps mouth and spike angles, and each spike's own damage, for facing checks. |
| `actor_state.gd` | Health and energy. Upkeep and movement drain energy; at zero the cell moves at half speed and loses health, and food ends it. Health regenerates above half energy. |
| `interaction_resolver.gd` | Feed, bite and ram: diet, reach, facing arc, size limit and cooldown. Only spikes facing the target count in a ram (strongest first, then diminishing); backing away is no ram; nothing can bite or ram itself. Damage includes the target's defense, applied once. A refusal names the missing requirement and changes nothing. |
| `ecology_region.gd` | Plankton zones with capacity and logistic regrowth plus a floor, so an empty zone always recovers. Seeded spawn points. Niche caps by role. |
| `editor_session.gd` | Working copy with undo, redo, mirror, move, scale; cost of additions minus refunds; preview equals commit; a failed commit keeps the original body and points. |
| `lineage.gd` | Found a lineage from a starting body (grazer, hunter, omnivore); reproduction needs 70% energy and a session opened with the save's current points, and records generation n+1; death keeps the body and loses a quarter of unspent points; points are earned from food and one per 30 s alive. |
| `save_game.gd` + `systems/save_migrations.gd` | Save schema v2 adds adaptation points; v1 saves migrate in memory. A missing or wrong-typed field in a save (`json_values.gd`) fails validation, so the backup is used and is not overwritten by the damaged file. |

All numbers are PROPOSED tuning (`game/data/part_catalog.json` and the constants at the top of each file).

### What the player gets
- The game opens on a start screen: Continue, New lineage and Quit, with the build id at the bottom right. The Continue card shows the body, lineage tag, generation, points, saved time and a preview of the cell.
- New lineage shows three body cards: grazer, hunter and omnivore. Each has a role, a diet and stats taken from the rules: speed, turning, upkeep, sight and max energy. One shared preview shows the selected body.
- The tide pool is one round pool 80 m across with 18 rocks and 7 plankton zones. The cell swims on one flat plane. The camera looks down at 55 degrees, always faces north and follows with a short look-ahead. The mouse wheel zooms between 10 and 20 m.
- Controls: WASD or the arrow keys swim in screen directions; holding the left mouse button swims toward the cursor. Esc or P pauses. F11 toggles fullscreen. Eating happens on contact; there is no eat key.
- Two foods. Plankton drifts inside its zone and regrows from the zone's stock; a filter mouth eats it. Carrion lies still and rots after 120 s; a jaw eats it. The grazer has a filter mouth, the hunter a jaw, the omnivore both.
- For now carrion also appears by itself in the zones. This is interim: the plan is for step 3 to turn it off, so carrion comes only from kills. (Step 3 did: see below.)
- Every meal goes through the step 1 rules. When food touches the cell but cannot be eaten, a hint says why: "Needs a jaw: your filter mouth only eats plankton", "Needs a filter mouth: your jaw only eats carrion", "Turn your mouth toward it" or "Full". With a diet hint the cell also shakes its head. Every hint except "Full" plays a sound.
- HUD: an energy bar with a Fed, Hungry or Starving chip; a health bar while hurt or starving; the lineage line; adaptation points with a +N flash; meal toasts such as "+8 energy · +1 point"; one survival point per 30 s alive; a controls hint until the first meal or for 30 s.
- Starving: a banner with an icon and text, half speed, the health bar, a grey cell and an arrow to the nearest food this body can eat.
- Death (interim): an "Out of energy" notice says the lineage continues and names the points it costs (a quarter of the unspent points). Continue puts the cell back at the start with full health. The world keeps going.
- Pause menu: Resume, Save, Save and quit to title, Quit to desktop. Quitting or closing the window with unsaved changes asks "Save first?". The footer shows the seed and build.
- Continue brings back the lineage, body, generation, points and place. Energy and health are not saved, so the cell comes back rested (60% energy, full health) and the HUD says so.
- Placeholder sounds are made in code at startup (eating, refusal, a starving pulse, ambience). Every sound except the ambience has a visual cue.
- Blender blockouts for the pool floor, three rocks, plankton, carrion and four parts (filter mouth, jaw, flagellum, eye spot). Each has a `.blend`, a GLB and a record. They are PROPOSED and not visually approved.

**Dev build only** (`Pangaea-dev.exe` and runs from the editor; none of this is in `Pangaea.exe`):
- F3 opens the debug panel: build, ids, seeds, tick, step cost, FPS, draw calls, adapter, driver, actor and derived stats, the last feeding results, zones and carrion.
- F5 fills energy, F6 drains it, F7 empties the nearest zone, F8 drops carrion 4 m ahead. The panel also has Teleport to spawn, gizmos (zones, reach, mouth arcs) and a vsync toggle.
- `-- --perf-tour` lets the forage bot swim for 90 s after a 2 s warm-up, then writes `perf_<build>.json` to `reports/` in the user folder and quits.

## Step 3: threats

**State:** IMPLEMENTED on branch `m1-threats`; exported but not Windows-verified; owner review pending. The owner confirmed OD1 (save the death at once); OD2 is not raised and OD3 is open. The build has the recommended option of each (see "Owner decisions" below).

### What the player gets
- **Ambush hunters.** Three big, flat, dark cells with amber eyes wait at the south-east rim, the south-west rim and the south rim beyond the south zone (moved there from the north-west rock gap by the step 3 review). One that notices you shows a "!" and turns to watch you. A fed one only watches; a hungry one (below 60% energy) chases you within 9 m of its lair. If you are 2.5 to 3 m in front of it, it stops and coils for 1.2 s: it squashes, opens its jaw, lowers its brow, its eyes brighten, a growl rises and a lane appears under it. Then it lunges along the line it locked when the coil began.
- **Territorial rammers.** Four pale, banded, spiked cells guard the rich north zones: two in z_n, one each in z_nw and z_ne. Swim into a patch (the zone plus 2 m) and one notices you, swims to about 4 m from you, puffs up for 1.2 s (crest up, spikes raised, a hiss, a lane) and charges along a locked line. Leave its patch during the puff and it deflates and goes home. A charge into a rock dazes it for 2 s.
- **Every attack can be dodged.** Swim out of its line on the side you are facing (a turn of at most 90 degrees) within 0.4 s of the tell starting. The other side is not promised: turning all the way round takes longer than the 1.2 s tell, and facing the creature even a turn of about 100 degrees to that side is hit. A fed cell outswims every chase: ambush hunters pursue at 2.34 m/s and rammers at 1.84, below 0.9 × the slowest starting body (the omnivore's 2.72). Every chase ends: an ambush hunter gives up after 5 s of chasing without landing a hit, at its 9 m leash, or 2 s after it last sensed you; a rammer after 8 s, when you are 3 m past its patch, or 3 s after it last sensed you. Either one also gives up when its chase makes no headway for 2 s, and a rammer lets you go rather than puff at you more than 1 m past its patch. After any give-up it ignores you for 6 s (ambush hunter) or 3 s (rammer).
- **One at a time.** At most one creature chases or attacks you at once; the others watch or wait.
- **They sense only what they could.** Sight within a field of view (200 degrees for an ambush hunter, 270 for a rammer) up to 6 m, which rocks block; a touch sense that reaches about 1 m past its body all round; and pain. Hide behind rocks and nothing notices you; bump one from behind and it turns.
- **Fighting back.** A jaw bites and spikes ram automatically on contact; there is no attack key. A hunter or omnivore bite deals 8, so a rammer (37.5 health) takes 5 bites and an ambush hunter (45) 6. When contact cannot hurt, a hint says why: "Turn your jaw toward it", "Point a spike at it" or "Too big to bite".
- **Meat from kills.** An ambush hunter drops 4 chunks and a rammer 3, each 24 energy and 3 points. Carrion now comes only from kills and the three world-start pieces; the step 2 trickle is off, and two world-start pieces moved out of the rammer patches. Creatures hunt each other too: ambush hunters eat rammers that stray in front of them, and rammers attack any other cell that enters their patch, ambush hunters included.
- **Being hit.** The cell flashes red and flinches, a red vignette pulses at the screen edge, the health bar shows, a number rises ("−12") and a thump plays. An ambush bite deals 12 and a full charge at most 14.4, so no single hit takes half of a starting body's 30 health. A strike that misses you says "Dodged!".
- **Off-screen threats.** A creature that is noticing, watching, chasing or attacking you from off screen gets an arrow at the screen edge with its glyph (jaws for an ambush hunter, a star for a rammer); while it notices you the arrow shows a "!" (the "!" over a creature shows only on screen), and during a tell the arrow pulses and shows a "!". The notice chirp, the growl or hiss, bites, rams and a creature's death are positional sounds, heard up to 25 m away.
- **Death.** The notice names the cause ("Caught by an ambush hunter", "Rammed by a territorial rammer" or "Out of energy"), the points it cost, already taken (a quarter of the unspent points), "Saved." and a tip, such as "When one coils, swim out of its line on the side you face; turning round takes too long." A starving tip names only food the body can eat: a hunter is told "More carrion comes only from kills: bite a rammer from behind, jaw first, and keep biting until it dies, then eat what it drops." The hunter's card also says "Hunters must hunt to eat." The death is saved at once (OD1), so quitting at the notice asks nothing. If that save fails, the notice says "Could not save: …" and quitting asks "Your cell's death could not be saved. Quit anyway?".
- **A fair checkpoint.** Continue puts the cell back at the generation start: "Back at the generation start · sheltered for 5 s." While sheltered, threats cannot see or strike it, and a chip counts down "Sheltered 5". A death within 90 s of the last respawn gives 8 s, then 10 s, with 80% and then 100% energy instead of 60%. The killer ignores the cell for 30 s, no creature's leash comes within 12 m of the start, and a creature that is there anyway (only dev tools can put one there) is sent home. The lineage is never erased. A new lineage and Continue also start sheltered for 5 s.
- **Spawning by niche.** Creatures appear only at their own sites, at least 20 m from you and outside the widest view, fade in over 1 s and ignore everything for 3 s. A killed rammer's site refills after 30 ± 6 s, an ambush hunter's after 60 ± 10 s. Creatures and the shelter are not saved; Continue fills the sites again.
- Blender blockouts for the ambush body, the rammer body and the spike part (each a `.blend`, a GLB and a record, PROPOSED and not visually approved). The species' mouths, flagella and spikes are the player's part models placed on the bodies. The cell's own spike part now has a model, ready for the step 4 editor.
- The art team's production pass on those three (PR #9, merged into this branch): textured, normal-baked bodies at 2,922 and 3,428 triangles and the spike part, same ids, sizes and sockets (`docs/art/production_m1.md`). Each record says "production rev 1, awaiting owner look review".
- Nine new placeholder sounds made in code: notice, the lunge growl and puff hiss (as long as the tell), bite, ram, hurt, dodged, a creature's death and the respawn chime.

**Dev build only** (`Pangaea-dev.exe` and runs from the editor):
- F9 places a threat 6 m ahead (the species alternate) and F10 kills the nearest creature, which leaves its meat. The panel adds Freeze AI, Hide from AI and Hurt the cell 10.
- The debug panel's AI section lists every creature (id, species, site, state and phase with its timer, its target with how and when it last sensed it, its give-up timer, `[token]`, health, energy, distance from home and whom it ignores), then the niche counts, the respawn queue and the token, then the cell's shelter, death streak, last death and checkpoint.
- Gizmos: each creature's sight cone coloured by state, its touch circle, leash disc, patch and puff-cancel rings, lair, a line to where it believes its target is (dashed once that memory is old), the strike lane during a tell, the checkpoint's 12 m and 20 m circles, and the zoom-max view around the cell.
- Test drivers in `game/dev/`: EvadeBot (dodges a tell 0.4 s after it starts, out of the lane on the side the cell faces, or on the other side for the S5 measure; from sim events only), HuntBot (a hunter that hunts rammers from behind), KiterBot (circles creatures for the fuzz); ForageBot can keep clear of threats. The perf tour runs with the creatures live.

### Known issues
- **Cost.** With 7 creatures a tick costs about 560 to 830 µs under load here, above the plan's 600 µs early warning. The 24-creature stress gate's best-of-three reads about 1750 to 2010 µs, but a single run read up to 2386 µs against its 2500 limit, and the gate failed under load. Since the review fixes the limits are 1500 and 4400 µs, about 2.5 times a quiet reading, with the yardstick ratio catching a step that got twice as costly on a quiet machine or on CI; at load above about 15 here the microsecond checks still fail from load alone. Two plan remedies that change behaviour (line of sight for the nearest 4, 6 Hz idle thinks) are still available.
- **Suite time.** `check.sh` takes about 7.5 minutes here at load 5 to 6 (7 min 19 s on the merged branch), well over the plan's 4-minute budget: the review added four slow gates (the two far-side dodge grids, #32 pressure at about 90 s and #33 the camper at about 45 s), and every Godot run now has a time limit (`tools/bounded.sh`). `run_tests.gd -- --skip-slow` keeps local loops short.
- **Pressure.** Fed ambush hunters mostly watch, and nearly all fuzz tells came from rammers. HuntBot's hunter took no hits and made 5 to 7 kills in every 240 s gate run. The predation balance saw no creature kill another in four 5-minute runs, so its bound of 6 is barely exercised.
- **HuntBot at a carcass.** The gate's bot can stall beside a carrion piece it cannot turn its mouth onto at its slow approach throttle (seen once, seed 202 on the ai fix alone, before the lair_s move). It is a test-driver weakness, not a game rule; if the gate ever fails that way, make HuntBot back off and re-approach.
- **Brain by design.** A pursuer never tells on prey swimming straight away (the chase timer ends it), and an ambush hunter rarely tells on a hunter body grazing across at 0.98 m/s.
- Rammer patrol points lie up to 7 m from the patch centre, so legs of 12 to 14 m at about 0.83 m/s often run out of their 8 s navigation time and the rammer turns back mid-leg.
- The fuzz does not catch a missing leash clamp, tell leash guard or knockback clamp (other tests do), and it covers only one death of the cell.

From the 55 degree camera the rammer's tall dome hides some far-side spikes, and its raised crest's points render dark navy (the side the camera sees faces away from the key light) until the production bodies give them a faint threat-colour emission.

### What the player gets
- **No wall.** A new lineage starts in the M1 pool, now the home block of a sea that goes on in every direction. The rim is gone; the sea is built round the cell as it swims, and nothing pops in or out near it.
